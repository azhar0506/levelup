### What is the primary function of Helm in Kubernetes?

It simplifies the management of Kubernetes applicaitons by providing a way to easily install, upgrade, and manage applications on a Kubernetes clsiter. It uses tempalting engine to generate Kubernetes resources from templates, making it easier to manage complex applications.

### What is the purpose of Helm Charts in Kubernetes?

Faactilitate the deployment and management of applciations on a Kubernetes cluster. They provide a way to package, distribute and manage applicaitons, making it easier to dpeloy and manage complex applciaitons in a Kubernetes environment.

### How can Helm Charts be packaged for distribution?

Using the "helm package" command. This command takes a chart directory as input and generates a packaged chart archived that can be easily distributed and installed on other system
