### What is the primary function of NetworkPolicies in Kubernetes?

Used to classify Pods as isolated and control their communciation with other Pods and services. They provide a way to define network traffic rules that allow or deny communication between Pods based on labels, ports, and other criteria

### In Kubernetes, what does an 'Ingress' rule in a NetworkPolicy define?

An Ingress rule specifies the type of traffic that is aloowed to enter (or ingress) into pods from external sources or other pods

### How does a NetworkPolicy in Kubernetes become effective?

becomes effectice after it is applied. When a NetworkPolicy is created and applied to a cluster, it defined the rules for traffic fliow between pods and services. The policy takes effect immediatley after it is successfully applied, allowing or blocking traffic accroding to its specifications.

### Which component is essential for the enforcement of NetworkPolicies in Kubernetes?

A Container Network Interface plugin (CNI). This is responesible for configuring and managing the nerwork interfaces of containers, which include enforcing NetworkPolicies. When a NetworkPolicy is created, the CNI plugin is responsible for implementing the policy by configuring the necessry network rules to allow or deny traffic.

### What is the default behaviour of a pod in a Kubernetes cluster that does not have any NetworkPolicies applied to it?

By default, a pod in a Kubernetes cluster without any NetworkPolicies applied to it can send and receive traffic from any source. This means that there are not restrictions on incoming or outgoing traffic, allowing the pod to freely communicate with other pods, services and external networks.

### How do NetworkPolicies behave when multiple policies are applied to a set of pods in Kubernetes?

Their effects are additive and cumulative This means that each policy builds upon the previous ones, creating a more complex set of rules that govern network traffci to and from the pods. The resultign policy is the combination of all the individual policies.
