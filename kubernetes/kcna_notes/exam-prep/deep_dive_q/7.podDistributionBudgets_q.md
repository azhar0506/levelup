### What is the primary purpose of Pod Disruption Budgets (PDBs) in Kubernetes?

Primarily used to ensure high availability during voluntary disruptions, such as upgrades or maintenance. By specifying a PDB, you cna contorl the maximum number of pods that cna be terminated at any given time, ensuring that a minimum number of replicas are always available

### In the context of Kubernetes, what does the term 'voluntary disruptions' refer to?

Refer to intentional actions taken by administrators, such as maintenance or upgrades, that may cause pods to be terminated or beocme unavailable. These disruptions are planned and excuted by humans, hence the term "voluntary"

### What is the difference between PDBs and replicas in Kubernetes?

Replicas ensure that a specfied number of replicas of a pod are running at any given time, which helps amintain availability under normal operations. On the other hand, PDBs protect against diruptions by specifying the maximum number of pods that can be terminated within a certain time period, ensuring that a minumum number of replicas are available even during disruptions.
