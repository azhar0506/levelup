### What is the default time the Node Controller waits before evicting pods from an unreachable node?

5 minutes - This delay prevents unnecessary disruption due to short-live connectivity problems

### When a pod is evicted due to an unreachable node, what typically happens next?

A new pod is creted on another available node - thanks to scheduler


### What is an example of Kubernetes resources that are typically cleaned up by the garbage collector?

- Terminated Pods
- Completed Jobs
- Ophaned ReplicaSets
